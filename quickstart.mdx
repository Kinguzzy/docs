This guide walks you through everything you need to do to have K8dKeep fully operational for your childcare center. By the end, you will have a verified account, a configured center profile, at least one active classroom, an enrolled child, and your billing ready to collect payments — all in under 30 minutes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Your Account">
    Go to [app.k8dkeep.com/signup](https://app.k8dkeep.com/signup) and fill in the registration form:

    - **Center Name** — enter the legal or operating name of your childcare center.
    - **Your Email Address** — this becomes your administrator login and the address K8dKeep uses for important account notifications.
    - **Password** — choose a strong password of at least 10 characters.

    After you submit the form, K8dKeep sends a verification email to the address you provided. Open that email and click **Verify My Email** to activate your account. If the email doesn't arrive within a few minutes, check your spam folder or use the **Resend Verification** link on the login page.

    Once verified, log in at [app.k8dkeep.com](https://app.k8dkeep.com) to reach your Dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Up Your Center">
    From the Dashboard, navigate to **Settings > Center Profile** to complete your center's core information. K8dKeep uses these details on invoices, enrollment packets, and parent-facing communications, so fill them in carefully.

    Provide the following:

    - **Address** — your center's full street address, including suite or unit number if applicable.
    - **License Number** — your state-issued childcare license number. This appears on official documents and enrollment forms.
    - **Operating Hours** — set your daily open and close times. These feed into attendance reports and billing calculations.
    - **Center Logo** — upload a PNG or JPG (recommended size: 400 × 400 px). Your logo appears on invoices, digital enrollment packets, and the parent portal.

    Click **Save Changes** when you are finished. You can return to this page at any time to update your information.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a Classroom">
    Navigate to **Classrooms > Add Classroom** to create your first room. Each classroom in K8dKeep represents a physical space in your center and groups children by age or program type.

    Fill in the classroom details:

    - **Room Name** — a clear, descriptive name such as "Infant Room," "Toddler A," or "Pre-K Blue."
    - **Age Range** — set the minimum and maximum ages (in months or years) for children assigned to this room.
    - **Maximum Capacity** — enter the total number of children this room is licensed to hold.
    - **Staff-to-Child Ratio Target** — set your desired ratio (for example, 1:4 for infants). K8dKeep displays a real-time ratio indicator on the classroom dashboard and alerts staff when the ratio is at risk of being exceeded.

    Click **Create Classroom** to save. Repeat this process for each room in your center before moving on to enrollment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enroll Your First Child">
    Go to **Enrollment > New Enrollment** to add a child to your center and assign them to a classroom.

    Complete the enrollment form in three sections:

    **Child Details**
    - Full legal name, date of birth, and gender
    - Any allergies, medical conditions, or special care instructions
    - Authorized pickup contacts

    **Family Contact Information**
    - Primary guardian name, phone number, and email address
    - Secondary guardian details (optional)
    - Emergency contact outside the household

    **Classroom Assignment**
    - Select the classroom you created in Step 3
    - Choose an enrollment start date and program type (full-time, part-time, or drop-in)

    Once the form is complete, click **Send Enrollment Packet**. K8dKeep emails the family a secure link where they can review, complete, and digitally sign all required forms — including health history, photo release, and your center's policies. You can monitor the packet's status from the **Enrollment** dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If you are migrating from another system or a spreadsheet, you can import existing families in bulk using a CSV file. Go to **Enrollment > Import Families**, download the K8dKeep CSV template, fill in your data, and upload the file. K8dKeep maps each row to a new family record and flags any missing required fields before the import is finalized.
</Tip>
