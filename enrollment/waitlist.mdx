K8dKeep's waitlist feature lets you capture interest from families even when your classrooms are full, so you never lose a potential enrollment. Every family on the waitlist gets a timestamped record, a confirmation email, and a clear position in line — and when a spot opens up, K8dKeep automates the outreach so you fill vacancies faster with less administrative effort.

## Adding a Family to the Waitlist

Follow these steps to record a new family on the waitlist. The process takes under two minutes and automatically sends the family a confirmation email once you save.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Waitlist">
    Navigate to **Enrollment > Waitlist** in the left sidebar. You'll see a sortable list of all families currently waiting, along with their desired start dates and classroom preferences.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a New Entry">
    Click **Add to Waitlist** in the top-right corner. A slide-out form opens for you to enter the family's information.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Child Information">
    Fill in the child's full name, date of birth, desired start date, and preferred classroom or age group (e.g., Infant, Toddler, Pre-K 3, Pre-K 4). If the family has a second-choice classroom, you can record that as well.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Contact Information">
    Enter the parent or guardian's name, email address, and phone number. If there are two guardians, add both — K8dKeep will send notifications to all contacts on the record.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the Entry">
    Click **Save**. K8dKeep immediately sends the family a confirmation email with the child's name, the classroom they're waiting for, and their approximate position on the list. The family's record is now visible in your waitlist view.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Prioritizing the Waitlist

K8dKeep gives you flexible options for ordering your waitlist so spots are offered to the right families first. Click **Sort & Priority** at the top of the Waitlist view to choose your preferred ordering:

- **Date Added (default)** — families are listed in the order they joined the waitlist, the most common and defensible approach for licensing purposes.
- **Desired Start Date** — families whose requested start date is soonest appear first, useful for filling near-term openings quickly.
- **Sibling Priority** — families who have a sibling already enrolled at your center are automatically moved to the top of the list for their age group.

You can also **manually reorder** individual entries by dragging them within the list. Manual overrides are logged with the staff member's name and a timestamp, giving you an audit trail if a family ever questions their position.

## Offering a Spot

When a classroom has an opening — whether due to a graduation, a departure, or an increase in licensed capacity — you can offer that spot to a waitlisted family directly from K8dKeep.

Select the family you want to offer the spot to and click **Offer Spot**. Choose the classroom and the start date, set a **response deadline** (we recommend 48–72 hours), and click **Send Offer**. K8dKeep sends the family an email and SMS with the offer details and a direct link to accept or decline.

If the family accepts, their status automatically advances to **Application Sent** and you can immediately send them an enrollment packet. If they decline or the deadline passes without a response, K8dKeep flags the entry and prompts you to move to the next family on the list.

## Waitlist Notifications

K8dKeep handles outbound communication to waitlisted families automatically so your staff doesn't have to track it manually. Families receive notifications at each of the following moments:

- **Confirmation** — immediately when added to the waitlist
- **Position Update** — when their position changes due to another family being enrolled or removed
- **Spot Offer** — when you send an enrollment offer
- **Offer Expiring** — 24 hours before their response deadline

To customize the wording of any notification, go to **Settings > Notification Templates** and select the **Waitlist** category. You can personalize the subject line, body text, and sign-off for each message type while keeping K8dKeep's dynamic fields (child name, classroom, deadline date) intact.

<Tip>
  Enable the **Sibling Priority** toggle on any family record where a sibling is already enrolled. This flag automatically elevates the waitlisted child to the top of the list for their age group, honoring your center's sibling preference policy without requiring manual reordering every time a new family joins.
</Tip>
