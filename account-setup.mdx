Your K8dKeep account settings control how the entire platform behaves for your center — from the name that appears on invoices to the notifications your staff and parents receive each day. Taking a few minutes to configure these settings correctly from the start saves time and prevents confusion as your team grows and your families rely on the platform.

<Note>
  You must have an **Owner** or **Admin** role to access and change center-wide settings. Teachers and other staff roles can view their own profile settings but cannot modify center configuration.
</Note>

## Center Profile

Your center profile is the foundation of your K8dKeep account. The information you enter here appears on invoices, enrollment packets, the parent portal, and all official communications K8dKeep generates on your behalf.

To access your center profile, navigate to **Settings > Center Profile** from the left-hand navigation menu.

Configure the following fields:

- **Center Name** — the legal or operating name of your childcare center. This appears at the top of every invoice and enrollment document.
- **Address** — your full street address, including city, state, and ZIP code. K8dKeep uses this for billing documents and licensing compliance reports.
- **Phone Number** — your main center contact number. Parents see this in the parent portal and on automated communications.
- **Website** — your center's public website URL (optional). Displayed in the parent portal footer and on enrollment confirmation emails.
- **Logo** — upload a PNG or JPG file (recommended: 400 × 400 px, under 2 MB). Your logo appears on invoices, digital enrollment packets, and daily reports sent to parents.
- **License Number** — your state-issued childcare operating license number. Required for enrollment forms and inspection-ready reporting.

Click **Save Changes** after updating any field. Changes take effect immediately on all new documents; previously generated invoices or packets are not retroactively updated.

## Operating Hours

K8dKeep uses your operating hours to power attendance reporting, late pickup calculations, and billing proration. Keeping this information accurate ensures your reports reflect how your center actually operates.

To configure operating hours, go to **Settings > Operating Hours**.

- **Daily Hours** — set an open time and close time for each day of the week. Toggle individual days off for days your center does not operate (for example, weekends).
- **Closed Days** — add specific dates when your center is fully closed, such as federal holidays or center-wide professional development days. Closed days are excluded from attendance reports and can be used to automatically pause recurring billing.
- **Holidays** — create a named holiday list that repeats year over year (for example, Thanksgiving week or winter break). Holiday dates appear on the parent portal calendar so families can plan in advance.

<Info>
  Late pickup fees, if configured under **Billing > Fee Rules**, automatically calculate based on the close time you set here. Make sure your operating hours stay up to date whenever your schedule changes.
</Info>

## Notifications

K8dKeep sends email and in-app notifications to keep staff and parents informed. You can control which notification types are active and who receives them from **Settings > Notifications**.

The table below lists the available notification types and their default recipients.

| Notification Type       | Who Receives It        | Configurable |
|-------------------------|------------------------|:------------:|
| New enrollment          | Admin                  | Yes          |
| Payment received        | Admin                  | Yes          |
| Daily report sent       | Parents                | Yes          |
| Attendance alert        | Admin & Teachers       | Yes          |
| Invoice due reminder    | Parents                | Yes          |

To modify a notification, click the notification name in the list and use the toggle to enable or disable it. For configurable notifications, you can also adjust the delivery method (email, in-app, or both) and, where applicable, the timing — for example, how many days before an invoice due date the reminder is sent.

<Tip>
  Send yourself a test notification after making changes to confirm delivery is working as expected. Use the **Send Test** button next to each notification type in **Settings > Notifications**.
</Tip>

## Multi-Location Setup

If your organization operates more than one childcare center, K8dKeep's enterprise plan lets you manage all your locations from a single account. Each location has its own classrooms, staff, enrollment records, and billing configuration, while your Owner account provides a unified view across all sites.

To add a new location, navigate to **Settings > Locations** and click **Add Location**. Fill in the location's name, address, license number, and operating hours. You can then assign staff members to one or more locations and switch between locations using the location selector in the top navigation bar.

<Info>
  Multi-location management is available on the **Enterprise** plan. If you are on a Starter or Growth plan and need to add a second location, contact K8dKeep support or visit **Settings > Plan & Billing** to upgrade.
</Info>
