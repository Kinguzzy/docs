Classroom management in K8dKeep gives directors and teachers a single place to organize every room in your facility — from infant suites to school-age programs. You can assign children and staff to classrooms, track licensing ratios in real time, and build a detailed daily record of each child's meals, naps, activities, and developmental milestones, all visible to families the moment you save them.

## Classroom Dashboard

The classroom dashboard gives you an at-a-glance view of everything happening in a room right now. At the top of each classroom card you'll see the total number of enrolled children alongside today's check-in count, so you always know who is present. The ratio indicator — displayed prominently in green, yellow, or red — reflects the current staff-to-child ratio based on checked-in children and on-site staff. Below the ratio, a live activity feed surfaces the most recent logged events: the last meal served, the most recent nap entry, and any learning observations posted in the past hour. Tapping into the dashboard from any device opens the full room view where teachers can log new activities, review the day's timeline per child, and see any flagged health or allergy notes.

## Multiple Classrooms

Most childcare centers serve children across a wide age span, which means managing several rooms simultaneously — Infants, Young Toddlers, Toddlers, Pre-K, and School Age, for example. K8dKeep lets you create as many classrooms as your facility requires, each with its own age range, licensed capacity, required ratio, and assigned staff. Every room operates independently on the dashboard, so a lead teacher in the Bluebird Toddler Room sees only their children, while the director gets a bird's-eye view of all rooms at once.

As children grow, K8dKeep's **Age Up** workflow guides you through moving a child to the next room. When a child approaches the upper age limit of their current classroom, K8dKeep surfaces an Age Up prompt on their profile. You review the transition, select the destination classroom, choose an effective date, and confirm. The child's full history — activities, development notes, incident reports — moves with them automatically, and their previous classroom slot is freed for a new enrollment.

<Tip>
  Configure ratio alerts to warn you **before** you reach your licensed maximum, not after. Set your warning threshold at 80% of the legal ratio so you have time to call in a floater or move a child to another room before you fall out of compliance. See [Ratio Alerts](/classrooms/rooms-ratios#ratio-alerts) to get started.
</Tip>
