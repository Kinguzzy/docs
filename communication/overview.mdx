## The Parent App

The K8dKeep parent app is available on both iOS and Android, giving families a dedicated, always-accessible window into their child's day. Once a parent downloads the app and links their account, they receive instant push notifications whenever a new message arrives, a daily report is published, or a payment reminder is due.

Beyond notifications, the parent app lets families take meaningful action in real time. Parents can check their child in or out directly from their phone, view activity photos as staff upload them throughout the day, and reply to messages from teachers without ever leaving the app. All interactions sync instantly with the K8dKeep dashboard your staff monitors, so nothing gets lost between a parent's phone and your classroom.

<Tip>
  Encourage every family to download the parent app during the enrollment process. Setting this expectation upfront ensures parents receive daily reports from day one and never miss an important announcement.
</Tip>

## Privacy & Data

Every piece of communication sent through K8dKeep stays entirely within the platform. Messages are encrypted in transit and at rest, and only authorized users associated with a child's account can view that child's photos, reports, and records. Photos shared in daily reports or activity feeds are visible exclusively to the child's linked family members — they are never posted publicly, shared with other families, or indexed outside of K8dKeep.

Staff members see only the children and families in their assigned classrooms, and administrators control which staff roles have access to messaging features. This layered permission model keeps sensitive family data secure while still making day-to-day communication fast and effortless.
