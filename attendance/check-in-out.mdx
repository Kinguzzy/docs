K8dKeep's check-in and check-out system replaces clipboards and paper logs with a tablet-based kiosk that families operate themselves and staff monitor in real time. Every arrival and departure is timestamped, linked to the child's profile, and visible across the platform — from classroom rosters to billing. Whether families check in independently with a PIN or a staff member assists, the record is the same: accurate, immediate, and permanent.

## Family PIN Check-In

Each family enrolled in K8dKeep receives a unique 4-digit PIN. This PIN is tied to their account and covers all children in their household. At arrival, the family enters their PIN on the kiosk tablet at your entrance, selects which children are arriving, and taps **Check In**. The child's status updates to present instantly, a check-in timestamp is recorded, and the classroom teacher receives a notification in the app.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Kiosk app">
    Launch the K8dKeep Kiosk app on your sign-in tablet. The app displays a PIN entry screen and should remain in kiosk mode at all times.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the family PIN">
    The parent or guardian types their unique 4-digit PIN on the on-screen keypad. The PIN resolves to their account and displays all children linked to that family.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the arriving children">
    The family selects the child or children who are arriving that day. Multiple children can be checked in simultaneously in a single session.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Check In">
    The family taps **Check In** to confirm. Staff receive a real-time notification, and the child appears as present on the classroom roster with an exact arrival timestamp.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Place the kiosk tablet on a stand near the entrance so families can self-check-in immediately upon arrival, without waiting for a staff member to be available. A mounted tablet at adult counter height works best for most centers.
</Tip>

## Staff-Assisted Check-In

If a family arrives without their PIN, or if you prefer staff to handle check-in directly, any authorized staff member can mark a child present from within the K8dKeep app.

Navigate to **Attendance** and select the appropriate classroom. The roster lists every child scheduled for the day. Tap the child's name and select **Mark Present**. K8dKeep records the current time as the check-in timestamp and updates the classroom roster immediately. Staff-assisted check-ins are flagged in the attendance log so you can distinguish them from kiosk check-ins if needed.

## Authorized Pickup Verification

Child safety at pickup is one of the most critical moments in the day. K8dKeep protects that moment by displaying the photo and full name of every person authorized to pick up a child before staff release them. If the arriving person is not on the authorized list, the system will not show a match — and the child should not be released.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Identify the pickup person">
    The pickup person arrives at your center and provides their name to a staff member.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the child's record">
    In the K8dKeep app, navigate to **Attendance**, select the classroom, and tap the child's name to open their record. Select the **Pickup** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm against the authorized list">
    K8dKeep displays the name and photo of each person authorized to pick up that child. Verify that the person in front of you matches the photo and name shown. If your center requires a photo ID check, complete that step before proceeding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Check Out">
    After confirming the match, tap **Check Out**. K8dKeep records the pickup time, the name of the authorized pickup person, and closes the child's attendance record for the day.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Never release a child to someone who is not on their authorized pickup list, even if that person claims to be a parent or guardian. K8dKeep displays the authorized list as configured by the enrolling family. If there is a custody order, emergency situation, or dispute, follow your center's written custody and release policy and contact your director before taking any action.
</Warning>

## Late Pickup Alerts

K8dKeep can automatically alert staff when a child remains checked in past your center's closing time or a configured pickup cutoff. To enable late pickup alerts, go to **Settings → Attendance → Late Pickup** and set a cutoff time for each program or classroom. After that time, any child who is still checked in triggers a notification to the assigned staff member and the center director. The alert continues until the child is checked out, ensuring no child is overlooked at the end of the day.
