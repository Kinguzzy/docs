K8dKeep turns every check-in and check-out into structured data your center can act on. From a live classroom roster that updates the moment a child arrives, to exportable history spanning months or years, the attendance reporting tools give directors, teachers, and licensing coordinators the information they need — without digging through paper logs or spreadsheets. You can spot patterns, document absences, verify billable hours, and satisfy state inspectors from a single screen.

## Real-Time Classroom Roster

To see who is currently in a room, navigate to **Attendance** in the main menu and select a classroom. The roster loads instantly and reflects the most recent check-in and check-out activity.

Each child's row is color-coded for fast visual scanning:

- **Green** — Child is currently checked in and present in the room
- **Grey** — Child is absent or has not been scheduled for today
- **Yellow** — Child is scheduled for today but has not yet arrived

The roster also displays the exact check-in time for each present child, making it easy for teachers to account for every child during headcounts and ratio checks. The roster refreshes automatically — no manual reload required.

## Attendance History

To review a child's attendance record over a specific period, open the child's profile from **Children** in the main menu. Select the **Attendance** tab within the profile. By default, the view shows the current month. Use the **Filter** controls to switch to a different month or define a custom date range.

Each entry in the history log shows the date, scheduled hours, actual check-in time, check-out time, total duration, and attendance status. You can use this view to verify billing accuracy, document a pattern of absences before reaching out to a family, or gather records for subsidy program reporting.

## Absence Tracking

K8dKeep distinguishes between two types of absences so your records accurately reflect what happened.

**Planned absences** occur when a parent notifies the center in advance — a vacation day, a medical appointment, or a scheduled day off. Parents can submit a planned absence directly through the K8dKeep Parent app, or a staff member can record it in the child's profile under **Attendance → Mark Absent**. When recording the absence, add a note (e.g., "Family vacation" or "Doctor's appointment") to keep the record descriptive.

**Unplanned absences** occur when a child does not arrive and no advance notice was given. If a child is scheduled but does not check in by a configurable threshold time, K8dKeep flags the absence automatically. Staff can then confirm and annotate the record, or reach out to the family to check in on the child's wellbeing.

All absence notes are visible in the child's attendance history and included in exported reports.

## Exporting Attendance Data

Exported attendance records are formatted for licensing inspections, state agency reporting, subsidy audits, and billing reconciliation. K8dKeep supports CSV export for data processing and PDF export for printed or emailed submissions.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Reports > Attendance">
    In the main menu, navigate to **Reports** and select **Attendance** from the report categories.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure your export parameters">
    Choose the classroom (or select **All Classrooms**), set your desired date range, and select your export format — **CSV** for spreadsheet-compatible data or **PDF** for a formatted printable report.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Export">
    Click **Export** to generate the file. K8dKeep compiles the report and downloads it to your device. Large date ranges may take a few seconds to process.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The exported file is suitable for presenting to licensing inspectors, submitting to state child care assistance programs, or cross-referencing with invoices during billing reviews.

## Attendance Report Fields

Every exported attendance record includes the following fields:

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| **Child Name** | Full legal name of the child as entered in their profile |
| **Classroom** | The classroom the child is assigned to on the record date |
| **Date** | Calendar date of the attendance record |
| **Check-In Time** | Timestamp of when the child was marked present |
| **Check-Out Time** | Timestamp of when the child was checked out |
| **Duration** | Total hours and minutes between check-in and check-out |
| **Status** | Attendance status: Present, Absent, or Late |

<Note>
  State licensing regulations specify minimum retention periods for attendance records — requirements vary by state and program type. Configure K8dKeep's data retention settings to match your state's requirements, and consult your licensing agency if you are unsure of the applicable retention period for your center.
</Note>
