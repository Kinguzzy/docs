K8dKeep's reporting suite gives directors and administrators instant visibility into every dimension of center operations — from whether this morning's attendance was fully signed in to how much subsidy revenue came in last quarter. Every report pulls from live data, so the numbers you see always reflect what's actually happening in your center. Whether you need a quick daily snapshot or a detailed export for a licensing inspection, Reports has you covered.

## Running a Report

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Reports Module">
    Click **Reports** in the main left-hand navigation. This takes you to the Reports home screen, which shows your recently run reports and any saved report configurations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a Report Category">
    Choose from **Attendance**, **Billing**, **Enrollment**, or **Staff** report categories. Each category displays the available report types within it — for example, Attendance includes Daily Sign-In Sheet, Monthly Summary, and Absentee Report.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply Your Filters">
    Use the filter panel on the left to narrow the report to exactly what you need. Depending on the report type, available filters include:
    - **Classroom** — one classroom or all classrooms
    - **Date range** — a preset range (today, this week, this month) or a custom start and end date
    - **Child** — a specific enrolled child
    - **Staff member** — a specific staff member's records
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate the Report">
    Click **Generate Report**. K8dKeep processes your filters and renders the report on screen, typically within a few seconds. For large date ranges or multi-location reports, generation may take up to 30 seconds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="View, Export, or Save">
    Review the report directly in the browser. When you're ready to share or archive it, click **Export** in the top-right corner of the report view and choose your preferred format:
    - **CSV** — best for importing into spreadsheets or accounting software
    - **PDF** — best for printing, emailing, or submitting to licensing agencies
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Compliance & Licensing Reports

K8dKeep includes a set of pre-built report templates designed specifically for the documentation that licensing agencies and state programs most commonly request. You can find all of these under **Reports > Compliance Templates**.

- **Daily attendance sign-in sheets** — formatted, printable records showing each child's arrival and departure time with a signature line. Meets requirements for most state licensing bodies.
- **Staff-to-child ratio logs** — hourly ratio snapshots for each classroom throughout the day, pulled from attendance and clock-in data. Useful for demonstrating continuous compliance during a site visit.
- **Medication administration records** — a log of every medication event recorded in K8dKeep, including the child's name, medication, dose, time administered, and the staff member who administered it.
- **Incident report summaries** — a compiled list of all logged incidents within a date range, including type, date, child, staff involved, and resolution notes.
- **Staff certification expiry list** — a full roster of staff with their current certification statuses, expiry dates, and flags for any expired or expiring-soon credentials.

All compliance templates can be exported as PDF for submission and as CSV for your own records. K8dKeep does not submit reports directly to licensing agencies on your behalf — always verify that the exported format meets your specific state or local requirements before submission.

## Scheduled Reports

Instead of manually running the same report every week or month, you can configure K8dKeep to generate and email a report to you automatically on a recurring schedule.

To set up a scheduled report:

1. Go to **Reports > Scheduled Reports** and click **New Schedule**.
2. Select the report type and configure your filters exactly as you would when running it manually.
3. Choose the frequency: **Daily**, **Weekly** (pick a day of the week), or **Monthly** (pick a date).
4. Enter one or more recipient email addresses — you can send scheduled reports to multiple Admins or Owners.
5. Click **Save Schedule**.

The report will run automatically at 6:00 AM in your center's configured time zone and deliver a PDF attachment to all listed recipients. You can edit or delete a scheduled report at any time from the **Scheduled Reports** list.

## Multi-Location Reporting

If your organization operates more than one childcare center, K8dKeep's enterprise plan gives you consolidated reporting across all locations from a single view.

Go to **Reports > Multi-Location View** and select **All Locations** (or a subset of locations) from the location picker at the top of the screen. Every report category supports multi-location filtering, so you can compare enrollment across sites, view combined revenue, or pull a system-wide staff certification status in one export.

Multi-location reports clearly label each row with the originating location, making it straightforward to break down results by site after exporting to a spreadsheet. Access to Multi-Location View is restricted to Owner and Admin roles.

<Tip>
  If you run the same report regularly — for example, a weekly billing summary filtered to a specific classroom — save it as a **Saved Report** so you don't have to re-enter your filters each time. After generating any report, click **Save Configuration** and give it a name. Your saved reports appear on the Reports home screen and can be rerun in one click.
</Tip>
