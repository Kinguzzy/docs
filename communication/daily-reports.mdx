Daily reports give parents a detailed, personal look at everything their child experienced while in your care. K8dKeep automatically assembles each report from the activities your staff logs throughout the day — meals, naps, diaper changes, learning activities, and photos — and delivers it directly to the parent app at a time you choose. No manual data entry at end of day, no missed updates, and no separate email drafts for staff to worry about.

## What's in a Daily Report

Each daily report is generated automatically from the activity data your staff records during the day. Parents receive a clear, organized summary that includes:

- **Meals and snacks** — what was offered at each meal or snack time, and how much the child ate (e.g., "Ate most", "A few bites", "Declined")
- **Nap and rest times** — when the child went down and woke up, and the total duration of rest
- **Diaper and potty log** — for infants and toddlers, a full record of diaper changes and any potty training progress noted by staff
- **Learning activities and milestones** — activities completed during the day (art, music, storytime, outdoor play) and any developmental milestones observed
- **Photos from the day** — images captured by staff and attached to activity logs, displayed in the report timeline
- **Health notes** — any parent-facing health observations logged by staff, such as a reported fever check or a minor scrape from outdoor play
- **A personal teacher note** — an optional free-text message from the classroom teacher that adds a warm, personal touch to the automated summary

## When Reports Are Sent

Reports are delivered automatically at the daily send time configured in your center's settings. The default send time is **5:00 PM**, but you can adjust it to match your closing schedule.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Communication Settings">
    Go to **Settings > Communication** from your admin dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the Daily Report Schedule">
    Under the **Daily Report Schedule** section, use the time picker to set your preferred send time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the 'No Activity' Option">
    Choose whether to send a report on days when no activities have been logged for a child. We recommend turning this **off** to avoid sending empty reports on days a child was absent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save Your Changes">
    Click **Save**. The new send time takes effect the same day if saved before the scheduled time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Daily reports respect each child's attendance record. If a child is marked absent, K8dKeep will not generate or send a report for that child on that day — regardless of your "no activity" setting.
</Info>

## Manual Reports

If a parent needs an update before the scheduled send time — or if you want to push a report immediately after a special event — you can trigger one manually at any point during the day.

Navigate to **Classroom > [child's name] > Actions > Send Daily Report**. K8dKeep will compile everything logged so far that day and deliver it to the parent right away. The scheduled end-of-day report will not send again for the same child on the same day unless you reset it manually.

## Parent View

In the parent app, daily reports appear as an interactive timeline of the child's day. Each logged activity shows the time it was recorded, a short description, and any attached photos. Parents can scroll through the full day at a glance or tap individual entries to see more detail.

Parents can also engage directly with the report — they can react to entries using emoji (a quick heart on a milestone photo, for example) and leave a comment that the classroom teacher will see in their K8dKeep dashboard. This lightweight back-and-forth keeps families connected without generating the volume of individual messages that direct messaging would require.

## Customizing the Teacher Note

Before the daily report sends, any staff member assigned to the classroom can add a personal note for a specific child. This is a great place to share a small moment from the day that didn't fit neatly into a structured activity log — a funny thing the child said, a friendship moment, or encouragement about a skill they're developing.

From the classroom view, tap **Add Teacher Note** under the child's name. Type your message in the text field and tap **Save**. The note will be included at the top of that child's daily report when it sends.

<Warning>
  Teacher notes must be saved before the daily report's scheduled send time. Notes added after the report has already been sent will not appear in that day's report — they will carry over to the following day's report instead.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  Use the **Group Activity** feature to log lunch and the afternoon snack for the entire class in a single step. Select the activity type, enter the shared meal details, and apply it to all checked-in children at once — saving your staff several minutes of individual data entry before the daily report sends.
</Tip>
