Family profiles are the central record for every child in K8dKeep — a single, secure location where your staff can find everything they need to know about a child and their family at a glance. From the classroom teacher checking an allergy before snack time to the director pulling a custody agreement at pickup, family profiles ensure the right information is always one search away and never buried in a filing cabinet.

## What's in a Family Profile

Each family profile is organized into labeled tabs so staff can navigate directly to the information they need without scrolling through unrelated fields.

**Child Details**
The core record for the child: full legal name, preferred name, date of birth, gender, profile photo, and current classroom assignment. You can also record the child's schedule (full-time, part-time, specific days) and their expected graduation or aging-out date.

**Family & Contacts**
A list of all parents and guardians associated with the child, including their relationship, phone numbers, email addresses, and home address. This tab also includes a **Custody Notes** field where staff can record any custody arrangements or court-ordered restrictions — visible only to staff with appropriate permissions.

**Medical Information**
A structured record of the child's health details: known allergies (with severity ratings), current medications and administration instructions, the child's pediatrician or primary care provider, and any special health needs or diagnosed conditions. This information is flagged prominently in the child's daily check-in view so classroom staff see it at sign-in.

**Authorized Pickups**
A list of every individual permitted to pick up the child. Each entry includes the person's name, relationship to the child, phone number, and an optional photo ID upload. Staff can mark certain individuals as **Excluded from Pickup** if a court order or parental request prevents them from taking the child.

**Documents**
A file library for all uploaded documents associated with the child and family. Common documents include immunization records, consent forms, custody agreements, insurance cards, IEP or 504 plans, and enrollment packet submissions. Files are stored with upload date and the name of the staff member or parent who uploaded them.

**Notes**
An internal staff-only notes log for observations, follow-up reminders, incident summaries, or any other information that should not be visible to parents. Notes are timestamped and attributed to the staff member who wrote them.

## Creating a Profile

You can create a family profile in two ways:

<Steps>
  <Step title="From a Completed Enrollment Form">
    When a family completes and submits an enrollment packet, K8dKeep automatically generates a family profile pre-populated with all the information they entered. Navigate to **Enrollment > Completed Packets**, open the submission, and click **Create Profile** to confirm. Review the imported data, assign a classroom, and save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manually">
    Go to **Families > Add Family**. Fill in the child's details, add at least one parent or guardian contact, and click **Save**. The profile is created immediately and you can populate the remaining tabs at any time. This is the fastest method when onboarding a family in person before sending a formal enrollment packet.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Updating Information

K8dKeep gives both staff and parents the ability to keep family profiles current, with different levels of edit access for each role.

**Parents and guardians** can log in to the K8dKeep Parent Portal to update their own contact information, add or remove emergency contacts, and update authorized pickup lists. Any change a parent makes is logged and triggers an in-app notification to the center admin for review. Parents cannot edit medical information, classroom assignments, or internal notes.

**Teachers** can view all profile tabs relevant to the child's care — including medical information and authorized pickups — but cannot edit records except to add entries to the **Notes** tab.

**Administrators and directors** have full edit access to all fields across all tabs. Admins can also lock specific fields from parent editing (for example, the authorized pickup list may require admin approval for changes).

## Document Storage

K8dKeep accepts the following file types for document uploads:

- **PDF** — recommended for multi-page documents such as IEPs, enrollment packets, and custody agreements
- **JPG / JPEG** — suitable for photo IDs and scanned single-page documents
- **PNG** — suitable for photos and scanned documents

The maximum file size per upload is **10 MB**. There is no limit on the number of documents stored per family profile.

If a required document is missing or expired (for example, an immunization record that needs annual renewal), navigate to the **Documents** tab and click **Request Document**. K8dKeep sends the family an email with a secure upload link — they can attach the file directly without logging into the portal. Once uploaded, the document appears in the profile and the request is marked resolved.

<Warning>
  Medical information — including allergies, medications, and special health needs — must be kept current to ensure child safety. Review every child's **Medical Information** tab at the start of each program year and after any reported change from a parent. Consider requiring families to re-confirm medical details during annual re-enrollment by including the Medical & Allergy Info section in your re-enrollment form template. Outdated medical records can create serious liability and, more importantly, put children at risk.
</Warning>
