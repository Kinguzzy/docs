K8dKeep's online forms replace paper enrollment packets entirely, eliminating the back-and-forth of printing, scanning, and chasing down missing signatures. You build reusable form templates once, then send a personalized enrollment packet to each family with a single click. Families complete everything on their phone, tablet, or desktop — and your team sees completion status update in real time without touching a single sheet of paper.

## Creating an Enrollment Packet

Before you can send forms to families, you need at least one form template. Templates are reusable, so you typically create one standard template per program type (e.g., infant care, preschool, school-age) and reuse it each enrollment season.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Form Templates">
    Navigate to **Enrollment > Form Templates** in the left sidebar. You'll see a list of any existing templates and an option to create new ones.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a New Template">
    Click **New Template**. Enter a descriptive name such as "Standard Enrollment 2024" or "Summer Camp Registration." Choose whether this template is for new enrollments or re-enrollment of returning families.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Sections to the Packet">
    Add the sections your packet requires. Recommended sections for a complete enrollment packet include:
    - **Child Information** — name, date of birth, gender, home address
    - **Emergency Contacts** — up to six contacts with phone numbers and relationship
    - **Medical & Allergy Info** — known allergies, medications, physician contact
    - **Authorized Pickups** — names and IDs of people permitted to pick up the child
    - **Consent Forms** — photo release, field trip consent, sunscreen/medication authorization
    - **Payment Authorization** — ACH or credit card on file for tuition billing
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Fields">
    Within each section, drag fields to reorder them. Toggle the **Required** switch on any field to prevent families from submitting the packet with that field blank. Add custom fields using the **+ Add Field** button if you need center-specific questions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the Template">
    Click **Save Template**. The template is now available to assign to any inquiry or enrolled family. You can edit templates at any time — changes only affect packets sent after the update; in-progress packets are not altered.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Sending a Packet to a Family

Once your template is ready, sending a packet takes less than a minute.

Open the family's record from **Enrollment > Inquiries** (or **Enrollment > Waitlist** if the family is waitlisted). Click **Send Enrollment Packet**, then select the template that matches the child's program. Review the family's email address, add an optional personal message, and click **Send**.

K8dKeep immediately emails the family a secure, unique link to their packet. You can also click **Copy Link** to share the URL directly — useful if a parent is standing at your front desk and wants to pull it up right now on their phone. The link does not expire, and families can save their progress and return to finish later.

## Tracking Completion

The **Packets** tab on any family record shows the current status of every packet sent to that family. You can also view status across all families from **Enrollment > Packets**.

| Status | What It Means |
|---|---|
| **Not Sent** | A packet has not yet been sent to this family. |
| **Sent** | The packet was emailed or linked but the family has not opened it yet. |
| **In Progress** | The family has opened the packet and saved at least one section but has not submitted. |
| **Completed** | The family has submitted all required sections and provided all required signatures. |

Click **Send Reminder** next to any packet in "Sent" or "In Progress" status to fire an automated follow-up email to the family.

## Required Documents

Families can upload supporting files directly within the enrollment packet — no separate email attachments needed. Sections like **Medical & Allergy Info** and **Authorized Pickups** include a document upload field where parents can attach:

- Immunization records
- Custody agreements or court orders
- Insurance cards
- IEP or 504 plans
- Photo IDs for authorized pickups

Uploaded files are stored securely in the child's family profile and accessible to authorized staff at any time after submission.

<Note>
  E-signatures collected through K8dKeep enrollment packets are legally binding under the Electronic Signatures in Global and National Commerce Act (ESIGN) and the Uniform Electronic Transactions Act (UETA). Each signature is timestamped, tied to the signer's verified email address, and stored with an immutable audit log — giving you the same legal standing as a wet ink signature.
</Note>
