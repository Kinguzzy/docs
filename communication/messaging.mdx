K8dKeep's built-in messaging system replaces the mix of personal texts, email chains, and printed flyers that make family communication hard to track. Staff can send a private message to a single family, broadcast an announcement to an entire classroom or the whole center, and search through every conversation ever sent — all from the same place. Every message is securely stored, permanently archived, and tied to the child's record, giving your center a reliable communication trail that personal phone numbers simply can't provide.

## Direct Messages

Use direct messages when you need to reach a specific family privately — to share an update, ask a question, or follow up on a pickup arrangement.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a New Message">
    Go to **Messages > New Message** from the main navigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the Family">
    In the **To** field, search by the child's name or the parent's name. K8dKeep will suggest matching contacts from your enrolled families.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compose Your Message">
    Type your message in the text field. You can also attach files (documents, images, PDFs) up to **10 MB** per attachment by tapping the paperclip icon.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send and Notify">
    Tap **Send**. The parent receives an instant push notification on the K8dKeep parent app and an email notification if enabled in their preferences.
  </Step>
</Steps>

All replies from the parent appear in the same thread, keeping the full conversation in one place. Staff can view and continue the thread from any device logged into K8dKeep.

<Note>
  Messages sent through K8dKeep are retained as part of your center's official communication records. We strongly recommend using K8dKeep for all family communications rather than personal phone numbers or email — this protects both your staff and your families by keeping conversations documented, searchable, and tied to the correct child record.
</Note>

## Announcements

Announcements let you broadcast a message to an entire classroom or your whole center at once. Use them for closures, upcoming events, policy reminders, curriculum updates, or any news that multiple families need to hear.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a New Announcement">
    Go to **Messages > New Announcement**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Your Recipients">
    Select who should receive the announcement:
    - **All Families** — every family with an active enrollment at your center
    - **Specific Classroom** — all families whose children are enrolled in a selected classroom
    - **Individual Families** — a custom selection of specific parent accounts
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compose the Announcement">
    Write your message using the rich text editor. You can bold or italicize text, add bullet points, attach files, and include an event date if the announcement is tied to a specific occasion.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule or Send Immediately">
    To send right away, leave the schedule option blank. To send later, toggle **Schedule for Later** and choose the delivery date and time — useful for after-hours drafts you want delivered during business hours.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Tap **Publish**. K8dKeep sends the announcement to all selected recipients and logs it in the Message History.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Message Notifications

K8dKeep keeps everyone informed with multi-channel notifications, so no message goes unseen.

- **Parents** receive a push notification on the K8dKeep parent app and an email notification for every new direct message and announcement.
- **Staff** receive in-app notifications within the K8dKeep dashboard when a parent replies to a thread or sends a new message.
- **Notification preferences** are configurable per user. Parents can manage their email and push notification settings inside the parent app under **Account > Notifications**. Staff can adjust their notification preferences under **Profile > Notifications** in the dashboard.

<Info>
  Parents who have not yet downloaded the parent app will receive all messages and announcements by email only. Encourage families to install the app to ensure they benefit from instant push notifications.
</Info>

## Message History

Every message and announcement sent through K8dKeep is automatically archived and remains searchable indefinitely. To find a past conversation or announcement:

1. Go to **Messages > History**.
2. Use the filter options to narrow results by **family name**, **classroom**, **date range**, or **keyword**.
3. Click any result to open the full thread or announcement, including all replies and attachments.

Message History is available to staff with messaging permissions and to administrators. Individual families can view their own message history in the parent app under **Messages > Past Conversations**.

## Read Receipts

For announcements, K8dKeep automatically tracks which families have opened the message and displays a read receipt indicator on the announcement detail page. You'll see a count such as **"14 of 22 families have read this"**, updated in real time as parents open the announcement.

Read receipts are especially useful for time-sensitive notices — like an early closure reminder or an allergy alert — where you need confidence that the message has actually reached the families who need to act on it. If you notice a low open rate, you can send a follow-up direct message to the families who haven't opened the announcement yet.
