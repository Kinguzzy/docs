## How to Get Up and Running

Follow these four steps to go from a brand-new account to a fully operational center on K8dKeep.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account and set up your center profile">
    Sign up at [app.k8dkeep.com/signup](https://app.k8dkeep.com/signup) and verify your email address. Then open **Settings > Center Profile** to add your center's name, address, license number, operating hours, and logo. A complete profile ensures your documents, invoices, and parent-facing communications are correctly branded from the start.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add classrooms and enroll your first families">
    Navigate to **Classrooms > Add Classroom** to create your rooms, set age ranges, and define capacity limits. Once your classrooms are ready, go to **Enrollment > New Enrollment** to add children, collect family contact details, and assign each child to a room. You can send digital enrollment packets to families directly from the platform.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up billing and payment collection">
    Open **Billing > Settings** to configure your tuition rates, billing cycles, and accepted payment methods. K8dKeep supports ACH bank transfers and major credit cards. Once configured, invoices generate automatically based on each child's enrollment plan, so you never have to chase a payment manually.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite staff and start tracking attendance">
    Go to **Staff > Invite Staff** and send role-based invitations to your teachers and administrators. Staff members can immediately begin using the mobile or web app to record check-ins and check-outs, submit daily activity reports, and communicate with families — all in real time.
  </Step>
</Steps>
