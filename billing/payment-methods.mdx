K8dKeep makes it easy for families to pay tuition online — no checks, no cash, and no trips to the front desk. Families can pay directly through the K8dKeep Parent Portal using a bank account or card, and they can enroll in autopay to have charges processed automatically on each invoice's due date. As a center admin, you get real-time payment confirmation and a complete payment history without any manual reconciliation.

## Supported Payment Methods

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="ACH / Bank Transfer">
    Families link their checking or savings account directly to their K8dKeep account. ACH transfers carry the **lowest processing fees** of any payment method, making them a cost-effective option for both your center and the families you serve.

    Keep in mind that ACH payments typically take **2–3 business days** to settle. The invoice will show a "Payment Processing" status during this window and update to **Paid** once the transfer completes. K8dKeep sends the family a confirmation email when the payment is initiated and again when it settles.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Credit & Debit Card">
    K8dKeep accepts all major credit and debit cards, including **Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover**. Card payments provide families with instant payment confirmation, and the invoice status updates to **Paid** immediately upon successful authorization.

    Standard card processing fees apply. Your center admin configures whether these fees are passed through to families at checkout or absorbed by the center. See the [Processing Fees](#processing-fees) section below for details.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Autopay">
    Autopay allows families to authorize K8dKeep to charge their saved payment method automatically on each invoice's due date. Once enrolled, families never need to log in to pay — the charge happens in the background and they receive a receipt by email.

    Autopay works with both ACH bank accounts and credit or debit cards. Centers that promote autopay enrollment during the onboarding process see a measurable reduction in late and missed payments. Families can cancel or update their autopay method at any time from the Parent Portal.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Setting Up Autopay

Families complete autopay enrollment in just a few steps from their Parent Portal account.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to the Parent Portal">
    Direct families to your center's K8dKeep Parent Portal link and have them sign in with their account credentials. New families receive a setup invitation by email when you complete their enrollment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Payments > Payment Methods">
    From the parent dashboard, click **Payments** in the top navigation, then select **Payment Methods** from the dropdown menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a payment method">
    Click **Add Payment Method**. To add a bank account, select **Bank Account (ACH)** and follow the prompts to securely link their account. To add a card, select **Credit / Debit Card** and enter the card details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable Autopay">
    After saving the payment method, toggle **Enable Autopay** to the on position. The parent selects which saved payment method to use for autopay if they have more than one on file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Autopay charges on the invoice due date">
    From this point forward, K8dKeep automatically charges the selected payment method on each invoice's due date. The family receives an email receipt after each successful charge, and the invoice status in both the Parent Portal and admin dashboard updates to **Paid**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Processing Fees

<Note>
  Processing fees vary by payment method — ACH transfers typically carry a lower fee than credit or debit card transactions. Your center admin configures fee handling under **Billing > Settings > Processing Fees**. Fees can be **passed through to families** (displayed as a line item at checkout) or **absorbed by the center** (invisible to families). Contact your K8dKeep account representative if you have questions about current processing rates.
</Note>

## Payment History

Both families and center administrators have access to a full payment history at any time.

**For families:** Log in to the Parent Portal and go to **Billing**. The Billing tab shows all invoices (paid, pending, and overdue), payment dates, amounts, and downloadable PDF receipts for each transaction.

**For center admins:** Navigate to **Billing > Payments** in the K8dKeep admin dashboard. From here you can filter payment history by child, date range, payment method, or status. You can also export payment records to CSV for use in your accounting software or for subsidy reporting.

<Tip>
  When you send a family their enrollment confirmation, include a direct link to the Parent Portal and a prompt to add a payment method. Adding this step to your standard enrollment workflow means families are ready to pay before their first invoice arrives — and you're far less likely to chase down payment details after the billing cycle begins.
</Tip>
