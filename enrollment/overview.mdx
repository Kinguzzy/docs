K8dKeep's enrollment module gives directors and administrative staff a single place to manage every family's journey — from the moment a parent submits an inquiry to the day their child's spot is confirmed. Whether you run a small home daycare or a multi-site preschool network, the enrollment pipeline keeps your team organized, reduces manual follow-up, and ensures no family slips through the cracks.

## Enrollment Pipeline

Every family moves through a defined set of stages so your team always knows where each inquiry stands. K8dKeep automatically timestamps each transition and logs staff activity at every step.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Inquiry">
    A parent submits an inquiry online, by phone, or via walk-in. Staff log the inquiry in K8dKeep and a family record is created. An automated acknowledgment email is sent to the parent immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Waitlist">
    If no classroom spot is immediately available, the child is added to the waitlist. The family's position, desired start date, and preferred age group are tracked until a spot opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Application Sent">
    Once a spot is available — or for centers with open enrollment — staff send a digital enrollment packet to the family. The packet status is visible to everyone on your team in real time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Forms Completed">
    The family completes all required sections of the enrollment packet online, uploads supporting documents, and provides e-signatures. K8dKeep flags any missing required fields automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enrolled">
    An admin reviews and approves the completed packet. The child's status moves to **Enrolled**, their classroom is assigned, and billing and attendance tracking are activated automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Enrollment Dashboard

The Enrollment Dashboard is your command center for all active enrollment activity. Navigate to **Enrollment > Dashboard** to see a live summary of pipeline counts — how many families are at each stage, how many packets are awaiting completion, and how many applications are pending admin review.

The dashboard also surfaces recent activity: new inquiries submitted in the last 48 hours, packets opened or completed by families, and any enrollment offers approaching their response deadline. Use the quick-filter buttons at the top to narrow the view by classroom, age group, or enrollment stage. Clicking any family's name opens their full profile directly from the dashboard.

<Tip>
  If you are migrating from another system or a spreadsheet, use the **Bulk CSV Import** feature under **Enrollment > Import Families** to bring in existing family records all at once. Download the CSV template, populate it with your current data, and upload — K8dKeep will create inquiry records for every row and flag any formatting issues before the import completes.
</Tip>
