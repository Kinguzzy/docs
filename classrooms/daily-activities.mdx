Daily activity logging in K8dKeep creates a living record of each child's day — one that teachers build in real time and families receive automatically. Every meal served, every nap taken, and every learning moment captured flows instantly into the parent's daily report feed, giving families the visibility and reassurance they want while giving your center the documentation it needs for licensing inspections, incident follow-up, and developmental tracking over time.

## Activity Types

K8dKeep supports six categories of loggable activities. Parent visibility is set at the category level and cannot be overridden on individual entries.

| Activity | Description | Shared with Parents |
|---|---|---|
| **Meals & Snacks** | Food offered, amount eaten, and any allergy flags triggered during service | Yes |
| **Nap / Rest** | Start time, end time, and total duration of rest periods | Yes |
| **Diaper / Potty** | Time of change, type (wet, soiled, dry), and optional staff notes | Yes |
| **Learning Activity** | Developmental skill area, observation description, and optional photos | Yes |
| **Incident / Injury** | Description of the incident, action taken, and parent notification record | Admin only |
| **Health Note** | Symptoms observed, temperature reading, medication administered | Admin only |

Incident and Health Note entries are visible only to staff with Admin or Director roles. They appear in the child's permanent record and in licensing export reports, but they do not appear in the parent's daily report feed.

## Logging an Activity

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your classroom in the K8dKeep app">
    Launch the K8dKeep app on your tablet or phone and tap **Classrooms**. Select your room to open the live classroom roster showing all checked-in children.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the child">
    Tap the child's name or photo on the roster. This opens their individual daily timeline, showing all activities logged for them today.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the activity type">
    Tap the **+ Log Activity** button and select the activity type — **Meal**, **Nap**, **Diaper/Potty**, **Learning Activity**, **Incident**, or **Health Note**. The entry form adjusts automatically to show the relevant fields for the type you select.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the details and attach a photo">
    Complete the required fields for the activity. For meals, record what was offered and how much the child ate. For naps, enter start and end times or use the built-in timer. For learning activities, choose a developmental domain (Language, Motor, Social-Emotional, Cognitive) and write your observation. Attach a photo by tapping the camera icon — you can take a new photo or choose one from your device's library.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and send to parents">
    Tap **Save**. The activity posts immediately to the parent's daily report feed in their K8dKeep family app. Parents with push notifications enabled receive an alert as soon as the entry is saved.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Bulk Activities

Many activities happen for the entire class at the same time — lunch, outdoor play, circle time, a group art project. Instead of logging the same entry for every child individually, use the **Group Activity** feature to log once and apply to all.

To log a group activity, tap **+ Group Activity** from the classroom roster view (not from an individual child's timeline). Select the activity type, fill in the shared details, and then check off which children the activity applies to — by default, all currently checked-in children are selected, but you can deselect any child as needed (for example, a child who was napping during lunch). Tap **Save**, and K8dKeep creates individual activity entries for every selected child simultaneously, each with the same details and timestamp.

Group Activity entries appear in each child's daily report exactly as if they had been logged individually, so parents see a complete, personalized record.

## Photo & Video Sharing

Attaching photos to learning activity entries is one of the most valued features for families. When you log a Learning Activity and attach an image, the photo appears directly in the parent's daily report alongside your observation note.

All photos shared through K8dKeep are **private to the child's family only**. They are never posted publicly, never shared across families, and never used outside your center's K8dKeep account. Parents can save shared photos to their own device from the family app, but photos cannot be forwarded or shared to other families within the platform.

To attach a photo to any learning activity entry, tap the **camera icon** in the activity form before saving. You can attach up to five photos per entry. Video clips up to 60 seconds in length are supported on Learning Activity entries for centers on the Growth and above plans.

<Info>
  To protect child privacy, always obtain written photo and media consent from families before sharing images through K8dKeep. Store signed consent forms in each child's enrollment documents under **Enrollment > Documents**.
</Info>

## Incident Reports

When an injury, altercation, allergic reaction, or other significant incident occurs, K8dKeep's incident report workflow ensures the event is documented completely and the right people are notified immediately.

To complete an incident report, log a new **Incident / Injury** activity from the child's timeline. The incident form requires:

- **Description** — a factual account of what happened, where, and when
- **Action taken** — first aid administered, steps taken to ensure the child's safety, or other response measures
- **Parent notification** — the method and time at which the parent was or will be notified

When you save an incident report, K8dKeep automatically sends an **in-app alert** to all staff with the Director or Admin role, and it places a **parent acknowledgment request** in the family's app. The parent receives a notification prompting them to review the incident summary and confirm they have been informed. Their acknowledgment is timestamped and stored with the report.

<Note>
  Incident reports are permanently retained in the child's record and cannot be deleted. They are included in the licensing export available under **Reports > Licensing Documents**, making them readily accessible for state inspections or audits at any time.
</Note>
