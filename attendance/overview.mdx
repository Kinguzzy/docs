Attendance in K8dKeep covers the full arc of a child's day — from the moment a family walks through the door to the moment a child is safely picked up. Instead of paper sign-in sheets and manual headcounts, K8dKeep gives your staff a live digital record of who is present, who is absent, and who is authorized to pick up each child. Every check-in and check-out is timestamped, tied to a child's profile, and available for reporting, billing, and licensing compliance.

## How Attendance Works

The daily attendance flow in K8dKeep is designed to require minimal effort from families while giving staff complete visibility.

At **arrival**, a family enters their unique PIN on the sign-in kiosk at your entrance. K8dKeep immediately marks the selected children as present, records the exact check-in time, and notifies the classroom teacher. If a family needs assistance, any staff member can manually check in a child directly from the classroom roster in the app.

At **pickup**, the flow shifts to safety verification. When a person arrives to pick up a child, staff open the child's record and compare the pickup person against the authorized pickup list — complete with photos. Only after a confirmed match does staff tap "Check Out," which records the pickup time and closes out the child's attendance record for the day.

This end-to-end digital trail eliminates the ambiguity of handwritten logs and gives you an auditable record for every child, every day.

## Staff-to-Child Ratios

K8dKeep displays live staff-to-child ratios for every classroom directly in the attendance view. As children are checked in and checked out throughout the day, ratios update automatically. If the number of checked-in children in a room exceeds the configured ratio threshold for that age group, K8dKeep triggers an alert so you can reassign staff before a compliance issue arises. To configure ratio thresholds for each room, see [Rooms & Ratios](/classrooms/rooms-ratios).

<Note>
  Attendance records are retained for the period required by your state licensing regulations. Check with your licensing agency to confirm the minimum retention period in your state, and configure K8dKeep's data retention settings accordingly.
</Note>
