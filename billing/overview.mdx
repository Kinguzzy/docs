K8dKeep's billing module takes the stress out of tuition collection by automating invoice generation, payment reminders, and financial tracking. Whether you run a small home daycare or a multi-classroom preschool, K8dKeep gives you a complete picture of your center's finances in real time — so you can focus on the children in your care instead of chasing down payments.

## How Billing Works

K8dKeep follows a straightforward billing flow that keeps your finances organized from the moment a child enrolls to every payment received.

1. **Create fee plans.** Define your tuition rates, billing frequency, and any extra fees (such as late pickup or supply fees) once, and reuse them across as many children as you need.
2. **Assign children to plans.** Link each enrolled child to the appropriate fee plan from their profile. K8dKeep automatically pro-rates invoices for mid-period enrollment starts.
3. **Invoices generate automatically.** On the schedule you configure, K8dKeep creates and emails invoices to families. No manual work required.
4. **Families pay online.** Parents pay through the K8dKeep parent portal using ACH bank transfer, credit card, or debit card — or enroll in autopay to never miss a due date.
5. **Track payments in real time.** Your admin dashboard reflects payment status the moment a transaction processes, so you always know who has paid and who has an outstanding balance.

## Fee Plans

Fee plans are the building blocks of your billing setup. You can create as many plans as your center needs to reflect the different schedules and rates you offer.

**Billing frequencies** — choose from Weekly, Bi-weekly, or Monthly billing cycles to match how your center collects tuition.

**Rate types** — set a flat recurring tuition amount, or use hourly and daily rates for families with flexible or part-time schedules. K8dKeep calculates invoice amounts automatically based on the rate type you choose.

**Extra fees** — add optional line items to any fee plan, such as:

- **Registration fee** — a one-time charge applied to the first invoice
- **Supply fee** — a recurring or one-time charge for classroom materials
- **Late pickup fee** — a per-15-minute charge automatically added when a child is picked up after your closing time

Once a fee plan is saved, assign it to individual children from their profile's Billing tab. You can update a child's fee plan at any time, and K8dKeep will apply the new rates starting from the next billing period.

<Tip>
  Enable autopay for families when they enroll. Centers that encourage autopay enrollment at the time of registration see a significant reduction in late and missed payments — and spend far less time sending payment reminders.
</Tip>
