K8dKeep gives you a single place to manage everyone on your team — from the moment you send an invite through daily time tracking and annual certification renewals. Whether you run a single-room daycare or a multi-classroom preschool, the Staff module keeps your personnel records organized, your ratios visible, and your compliance documentation ready when licensing comes knocking.

## Inviting a Staff Member

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Invite Form">
    Navigate to **Staff > Team Members** and click **Invite Staff** in the top-right corner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Staff Details">
    Fill in the staff member's full name, work email address, and select their role from the dropdown: **Owner**, **Admin**, **Lead Teacher**, **Assistant Teacher**, or **Viewer**. Not sure which role to choose? See [Roles & Permissions](/staff/roles-permissions) for a full breakdown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the Invitation">
    Click **Send Invite**. K8dKeep immediately sends the staff member an email containing a secure link to create their account and set a password. The link expires after 72 hours — if it lapses, you can resend it from the **Pending Invites** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign to Classrooms">
    Once the staff member accepts the invite and logs in for the first time, assign them to one or more classrooms. You can do this from **Staff > [Staff Name] > Classrooms**, or from the classroom side under **Classrooms > [Room Name] > Staff**. Staff cannot view or interact with a classroom until they are assigned to it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Staff Profiles

Every staff member in K8dKeep has a dedicated profile that stores everything you need for HR records and licensing audits in one place. Profiles include:

- **Personal information** — full name, profile photo, job title, and preferred contact information
- **Emergency contact** — name, relationship, and phone number
- **Employment details** — hire date and employment type (full-time or part-time)
- **Certifications** — CPR certification, First Aid certification, and state-required training hours, each with their own expiry date and supporting document upload
- **Background check** — upload date and document attachment for verification records

You can edit any profile field at any time by going to **Staff > [Staff Name] > Edit Profile**. Changes are saved immediately and logged in the profile's audit history.

## Classroom Assignments

Assigning staff to the right classrooms ensures they see only the children and families relevant to their role, and keeps your staff-to-child ratio tracking accurate.

To assign or reassign a staff member, use either path:

- **From the staff side:** Go to **Staff > [Staff Name] > Classrooms**, click **Add Classroom**, and select one or more rooms from the list.
- **From the classroom side:** Go to **Classrooms > [Room Name] > Staff**, click **Add Staff Member**, and select from your active team.

A single staff member can be assigned to multiple classrooms simultaneously — useful for floaters or administrators who cover the whole center. To remove an assignment, click the **×** next to the classroom name in either view.

## Time Tracking

K8dKeep includes built-in time tracking so you can record, review, and export staff hours without a separate system.

Staff members clock in and out directly from the K8dKeep mobile app or the web dashboard. Each punch is timestamped and tied to their profile automatically. Admins can view a live snapshot of who is currently clocked in from **Staff > Timesheets > Live View**.

To review or edit recorded hours, go to **Staff > Timesheets**, select a staff member, and choose the date range you want to review. Admins can correct missed punches or adjust times by clicking **Edit** next to any timesheet entry and entering the correct time along with a brief note explaining the change.

When you're ready to process payroll, export timesheets as a CSV from **Staff > Timesheets > Export**. You can filter by individual staff member, classroom, or date range before exporting.

## Certifications & Compliance

Keeping certifications current is one of the most critical — and easy-to-overlook — parts of childcare licensing compliance. K8dKeep helps you stay ahead of expiry dates automatically.

To upload a certification document for a staff member:

1. Go to **Staff > [Staff Name] > Certifications**.
2. Click **Add Certification** and select the certification type (e.g., CPR, First Aid, Background Check, or a custom state-required training).
3. Enter the issue date and expiry date, then upload the supporting document (PDF, JPG, or PNG).
4. Click **Save**.

Once a certification is saved with an expiry date, K8dKeep automatically sends an alert to all Admins and Owners **30 days before the certification expires**, giving your team plenty of time to arrange renewal. You can also view the full certification status for every staff member at a glance from **Staff > Certifications Overview**, which flags any expired or soon-to-expire credentials in red and yellow respectively.

<Note>
  When a staff member leaves your organization, **deactivate their account** rather than deleting it. Go to **Staff > [Staff Name] > Edit Profile > Deactivate Account**. Deactivating preserves all historical records — attendance logs, timesheet entries, incident reports, and communications — that reference that staff member, keeping your audit trail intact. Deactivated accounts do not count toward your active-user limit and cannot be used to log in.
</Note>
